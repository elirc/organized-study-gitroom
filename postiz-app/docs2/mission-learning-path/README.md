# Mission Learning Path

This suite is an active training path. Do not read it like normal docs. Open the cited files, annotate the code, answer the checkpoint questions, and grade yourself against the rubric before moving on.

Recommended pacing:

- Tier 1: one or two missions per session. Your goal is orientation and vocabulary.
- Tier 2: one mission per session. Your goal is tracing cause and effect across files.
- Tier 3: one mission per longer session. Your goal is judgment: risk, tests, review, debugging, and ownership.

Self-grading rule: a strong answer cites source lines, names the behavior, and explains the consequence. A weak answer uses framework generalities without pointing to this repo.

This suite differs from normal documentation because every mission asks you to do something: trace, annotate, compare, review, debug, or explain. The app domain is social scheduling, so think like a dispatch desk: channels are destinations, posts are scheduled deliveries, integrations are connected routes, and Temporal is the dispatch machinery after the schedule is saved.
